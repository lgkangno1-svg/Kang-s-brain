# Overseas design reference notes — 2026-09-12

These references inform visual direction only; Korea Concierge does not copy layouts or branding.

- Six Senses: destination-led storytelling, large photography, calm editorial rhythm and sparse premium chrome.
- Airbnb Experiences: strong experience photography plus practical clarity around what the guest does and why it is worth choosing.
- VISITKOREA: inspiration should stay connected to itinerary planning and useful on-trip information.

Applied principle: combine premium editorial travel storytelling with a functional, trustworthy tool surface rather than turning the product into a static marketing page.
